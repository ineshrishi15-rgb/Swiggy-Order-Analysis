# Swiggy Order & Spending Analysis | Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyzes personal Swiggy food delivery orders using Microsoft Excel to understand spending patterns, ordering habits, restaurant preferences, coupon usage, and delivery performance.

The workbook includes data preparation, standardized restaurant and area names, derived fields, PivotTables, formulas, and an interactive dashboard.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze monthly order volume and spending trends.
- Calculate key performance indicators (KPIs), including total spending, average order value, and average delivery time.
- Identify frequently ordered restaurants and food items.
- Evaluate coupon usage and recorded discounts.
- Understand ordering patterns by day of the week, time of day, and food preference.
- Present the findings through an Excel dashboard to support data-driven decisions.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and standardization
- Formula-based derived fields
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- KPI calculations
- Dashboard reporting and data visualization

## Key Findings

### 1. Spending Trends
- Total spending across 62 orders in the 2024 analysis was ₹25,567.
- May recorded the highest monthly spending, at ₹10,369 across 23 orders.
- The second quarter recorded ₹14,147 in spending across 33 orders, compared with ₹11,420 across 29 orders in the first quarter.

### 2. Restaurant Preferences
- Rasayan and Subway were the most frequently ordered restaurants, with 8 orders each.
- The most frequently recorded food item was the Single Lamb Cheese Burger, appearing in 6 orders.

### 3. Ordering Behaviour
- Tuesday accounted for approximately 24.2% of orders, making it the most frequent ordering day in the analysis.
- Evening orders represented approximately 37.1% of orders, the largest time-of-day category.
- Non-vegetarian orders accounted for 60% of orders, while vegetarian orders accounted for 40%.

### 4. Coupon Usage
- SWIGGYIT was the most frequently recorded coupon category, representing approximately 37.1% of orders.
- Approximately 22.6% of orders were recorded without a coupon.
- The workbook tracks discounts and free-delivery discounts to help understand the role of offers in personal food delivery spending.

### 5. Delivery Performance
- Average delivery time was approximately 35.4 minutes.
- Average restaurant-to-customer delivery distance was approximately 3.15 km.

## Business Insights & Recommendations

- **Monitor monthly spending:** May had the highest spending in the analyzed period. Reviewing order frequency and average order value can help manage the food delivery budget.
- **Evaluate promotional offers:** Compare coupon-based orders with orders placed without coupons to understand whether discounts provide meaningful savings.
- **Review restaurant choices:** Track spending and order frequency across frequently used restaurants to identify opportunities to optimize expenditure.
- **Use ordering patterns for planning:** Evening orders were the largest time-of-day category. Planning meals in advance may help control discretionary food delivery spending.
- **Track delivery performance:** Monitoring delivery times and distances can help identify patterns in the personal ordering experience.

## Limitations

- This is a personal order-history analysis, not a representative study of all Swiggy users.
- Findings depend on the completeness and accuracy of the available order records.
- Spending and ordering patterns describe the analyzed period and should not automatically be generalized to future periods.
